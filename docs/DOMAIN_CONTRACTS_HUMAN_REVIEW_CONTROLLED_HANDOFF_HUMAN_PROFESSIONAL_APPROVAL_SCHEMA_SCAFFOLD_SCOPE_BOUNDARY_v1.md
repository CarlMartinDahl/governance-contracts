## 1. Purpose

This docs-only boundary freezes the exact smallest future JSON Schema scaffold
scope for the Human Review Controlled Handoff human/professional
approval-decision contract. It resolves the eleven representation and proof
questions left open by the tracked schema-readiness review without creating the
schema or changing package, validator-result, validator, governance,
cross-reference, admissibility, approval-effect, persistence, audit,
handoff/export, delivery, release, recipient, or runtime behavior.

The future schema remains a structural candidate contract only. It cannot
prove candidate or reference existence, fingerprint correctness, reviewer
authority, session or attestation validity, attempt order, freshness,
currentness, approval effect, handoff eligibility, delivery, release, product
readiness, or external-use authority. Human and professional review remain
release gates.
