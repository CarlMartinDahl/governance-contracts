## Purpose

This document records a prerequisite/boundary freeze for the new domain-first track
`SWE_BODELNING_DOLD_SAMAGANDERATT`. It is not implementation. It is not the
substantive legal doctrine contract. It prepares the repository for later legal/source
inventory and domain-contract work by recording the current absence of a distinct hidden
co-ownership domain contract.

The future product direction is neutral evidence structuring and dossier preparation for
possible hidden co-ownership / dold samäganderätt in Swedish bodelning or family-property
contexts. The model role is to organize digital material, evidence references, timeline
signals, uncertainty, and missing information into a neutral forensic/evidence dossier.
The model role is not legal decision-making.
