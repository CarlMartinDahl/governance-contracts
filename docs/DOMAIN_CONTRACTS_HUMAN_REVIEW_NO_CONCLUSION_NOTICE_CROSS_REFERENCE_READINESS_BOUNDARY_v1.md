## 1. Purpose

This docs-only boundary records a prove-only assessment of readiness to create
the separately reserved Human Review No-Conclusion Notice cross-reference
checkpoint. It distinguishes the completed structural candidate,
validator-result, validator, and package schema surfaces from the unresolved
cross-reference input, prerequisite, dependency, comparison, result, ordering,
lifecycle, observability, and implementation-proof semantics.

The controlling contract bounds a possible later checkpoint to six checks:

1. exact packet-reference equality across the No-Conclusion Notice candidate,
   Source Register, Review Chronology, Asserted Claim Matrix, Declared Packet
   Review Gaps, and Human Review Questions
2. membership of every notice `source_ref` in the separately validated Source
   Register
3. membership of every notice `chronology_entry_ref` in the separately
   validated Review Chronology
4. membership of every notice `claim_ref` in the separately validated Asserted
   Claim Matrix
5. membership of every notice `gap_ref` in the separately validated Declared
   Packet Review Gaps
6. membership of every notice `question_ref` in the separately validated Human
   Review Questions

The contract permits that bounded future comparison scope. It does not require
implementation, select a checkpoint contract, define a result contract, or
make predecessor checkpoints runtime dependencies.

This boundary creates no checkpoint, result schema, validator, adapter,
parser, registry, lookup, dispatch, persistence, API, route, provider, model
execution, UI, logging, telemetry, audit emission, product behavior, or
external-use authorization. It processes no real, private, source, case,
identity, authorship, or evidentiary material. Human/professional review
remains the release gate.
