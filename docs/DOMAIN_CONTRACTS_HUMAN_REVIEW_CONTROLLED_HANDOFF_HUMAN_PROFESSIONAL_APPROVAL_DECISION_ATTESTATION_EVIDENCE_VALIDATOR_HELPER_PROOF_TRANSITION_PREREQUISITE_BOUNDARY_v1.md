This docs-only prerequisite aligns ten historical decision-attestation
evidence proof files before the separately scoped internal validator helper
may be created. It transitions exactly twelve live assertion sites checking
the two reserved helper paths, for a total of thirty-two executed filesystem-
absence checks. One of those sites is the tracked five-binding cross-monitor
