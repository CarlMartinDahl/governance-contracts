## 1. Purpose

This docs-only boundary freezes the exact smallest future JSON Schema scaffold
scope for the Human Review Controlled Handoff Human/Professional Approval
Reviewer Role Evidence contract. It translates the nine Owner-selected
technical representation decisions without creating the schema, changing a
package export, creating a validator-result schema or validator, or changing
cross-reference, identity, role, authority, qualification, currentness,
admissibility, persistence, API, UI, or runtime behavior.

The future schema remains a structural candidate contract only. It may encode
the wrapper's own closed fourteen-field representation. It cannot prove
reviewer identity, role assignment, professional qualification, reviewer
authority, permission, policy authority, lifecycle currentness, approval
admissibility, handoff eligibility, release readiness, or external-use
authority.

This boundary processes no raw, private, source, case, identity-provider,
credential, qualification-provider, policy, or real-evidence material.
Human/professional review remains the release gate.
