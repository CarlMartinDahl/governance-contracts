## Non-Decisional Statutory Scope Notes

For source-orientation and no-overclaim caution only (not legal advice or doctrinal interpretation):

- `SFS 2026:852` enacted `4 kap. 7 b § brottsbalken` with two statutory branches: repeated violations across six statutory conduct categories when taken together are apt to seriously harm self-esteem, and enduring undue surveillance when apt to seriously harm self-esteem.
- `SFS 2026:852` also added `4 kap. 7 b §` as an underlying offence reference in `4 kap. 3, 4 a, 4 b, and 4 e §§ brottsbalken`.
- Economic violence (*ekonomiskt våld*) was not enacted as a separate statutory element in `4 kap. 7 b § brottsbalken` (`Prop. 2025/26:138`, `Bet. 2025/26:JuU39`); any legal assessment of whether economic conduct intersects another statutory branch remains strictly a human legal judgment.
- Preparatory and parliamentary materials note legal-certainty boundaries and the risk of misuse or weaponization in high-conflict or custody disputes; source-bound counter-context and missing-context preservation are mandatory governance concerns.
- Because `4 kap. 7 b §` entered into force on `2026-07-01`, Supreme Court (`Högsta domstolen`) precedent remains nascent and must never be invented or assumed.

This document does not quote long statutory text and does not convert statutory structure into executable offence elements.
