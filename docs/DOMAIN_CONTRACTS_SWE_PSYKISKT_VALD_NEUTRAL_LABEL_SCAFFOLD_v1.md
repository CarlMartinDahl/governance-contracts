The verified source pack for source-provenance purposes is:

- Current official consolidated `Brottsbalk (1962:700)`, specifically `4 kap. 7 b §` (*psykiskt våld*) and consequential amendments to `4 kap. 3, 4 a, 4 b, and 4 e §§`
- `SFS 2026:852` (*Lag om ändring i brottsbalken*), issued `2026-05-28`, published `2026-06-03`, entered into force `2026-07-01`
- `Prop. 2025/26:138`, *En särskild straffbestämmelse för psykiskt våld*
- `Bet. 2025/26:JuU39` and `Rskr. 2025/26:257`
- `Ds 2022:18`, remissmaterial, `Ju2025/01157`, lagrådsremiss, and Lagrådet opinion
- Åklagarmyndigheten, Utvecklingscentrum, `Rättslig vägledning 2026:7`, *Brott i nära relation, olaga förföljelse och psykiskt våld* (June 2026, Chapter 4) as non-controlling official prosecutorial context
- Official case-law bucket (`Högsta domstolen` and `hovrätter`) not selected now and requiring later manual legal review

Source-priority model:

1. Current official consolidated `Brottsbalk (1962:700)` text first.
2. Enacted amending law `SFS 2026:852` (and any later SFS amendment) next.
3. Proposition, betänkande, and riksdagsskrivelse next.
4. Ds, remissmaterial, lagrådsremiss, and Lagrådet opinion next.
5. Åklagarmyndigheten `Rättslig vägledning 2026:7` and later official guidance as non-controlling official context.
6. Official case-law bucket only as later verified source candidate, not doctrine application now.

The source-priority model does not decide law, does not apply sources to facts, and does not select any outcome.

## Label Classification Rules

`SWE_PSYKISKT_VALD_LABEL_SCAFFOLD` may identify the neutral Swedish label-scaffold surface.

`SWE_PSYKISKT_VALD_CONTEXT_LABEL` may identify generic context for Swedish psychological-violence source organization without case application.

`SOURCE_PROVENANCE_LABEL` may identify official Swedish source provenance without making doctrine findings.

`TRANSLATION_CAUTION_LABEL` may identify translation risk between Swedish, Danish, and English legal terms.

`MANUAL_LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` may identify that human legal review is required before doctrine, application, or later modelling.

`NO_CASE_APPLICATION` may identify that no private facts are evaluated.

`NO_OFFENCE_DETERMINATION` may identify that no offence determination is made.

`MALSAGANDE_OR_AFFECTED_PERSON_CONTEXT_LABEL` may identify an asserted affected-person role in source organization, not a legal finding that any person is legally *målsägande* or a crime victim.

`ASSERTED_ACTOR_OR_COUNTERPARTY_CONTEXT_LABEL` may identify an asserted actor or counterparty role in source organization, not a legal finding of perpetrator status or guilt.

`ACCUSATION_OR_DISPARAGEMENT_CONTEXT` may group supplied material for human review near the *beskyllning* / *nedsättande uttalande* working area without deciding whether any statement is unlawful or constitutes a statutory violation.

`HUMILIATION_BEHAVIOR_CONTEXT` may group supplied material for human review near the *förödmjukande beteende* working area without deciding normative humiliation or statutory satisfaction.

`THREAT_OR_COERCION_CONTEXT` may group supplied material for human review near the *otillbörligt hot* / *otillbörligt tvång* working area without deciding whether conduct is *otillbörligt* or unlawful.

`MONITORING_OR_SURVEILLANCE_CONTEXT` may group supplied material for human review near the *otillbörlig övervakning* working area without deciding whether monitoring is *otillbörlig* or *varaktig*.

`REPETITION_OR_DURATION_TIMELINE_CONTEXT` may organize timestamps, sequence spans, and declared timeline gaps for human review without deciding whether *upprepat* or *varaktig* is satisfied.

`RELATION_AND_COUNTER_CONTEXT` may preserve relationship setting, surrounding thread context, counter-context, and selection-bias/missing-message warnings without making credibility or legal conclusions.

`ADJACENT_STATUTORY_CROSS_REFERENCE_CONTEXT` may record a non-decisional cross-reference note that `SFS 2026:852` also touched `4 kap. 3, 4 a, 4 b, and 4 e §§ brottsbalken`, strictly for human legal routing and never as an offence classification.
