## Review Checklist Sections

### Closed-Layer Coherence

- Verify that the review subject is a closed domain chain, not an open legal or runtime implementation.
- Verify that every listed closed layer has a named scope and closure commit.
- Verify that the review template does not reopen, merge, replace, or extend any closed layer.
- Verify that the chain reads coherently from boundary/source inventory through dossier scaffolding, labels, evidence-to-label boundaries, source-status, issue relevance, working ledger, corpus workflow, appendix/submission workflow, rebuttal matrix, and side-track separation.

### Boundary And Prerequisite Closure

- Verify that prerequisite closure remains a boundary record only.
- Verify that legal/source inventory remains an inventory and manual-review gate, not legal doctrine.
- Verify that neutral evidence dossier scaffolding remains organizational and non-decisional.
- Verify that doctrine/requisite labels remain labels and do not prove any requisite.

### Evidence Discipline

- Verify that working notes, party assertions, counterparty assertions, appendices, submission references, source links, issue links, and side-track entries are not treated as proof by themselves.
- Verify that traceability links remain traceability links.
- Verify that source-backed does not mean core-relevant.
- Verify that core-relevant does not mean legally proven.
- Verify that the template does not infer automatic legal conclusions from evidence material.

### Source-Status Boundaries

- Verify that source-status surfaces remain organizational and non-decisional.
- Verify that source-status review checks do not perform source-status classification.
- Verify that source-status references are not proof by themselves.
- Verify that uncertain source status remains `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

### Issue-Relevance Boundaries

- Verify that issue-relevance surfaces remain organizational and non-decisional.
- Verify that issue-relevance review checks do not perform issue relevance classification.
- Verify that issue placement is not legal conclusion, proof, sufficiency, or ownership determination.
- Verify that off-core, access/logistics, conflict/person, and side-track material does not contaminate the hidden-co-ownership core.

### Working Memo / Issue Ledger Boundaries

- Verify that working memo / issue ledger material remains working-note and issue-tracking material only.
- Verify that working notes do not become verified evidence without source-status and issue-relevance gates.
- Verify that hypotheses, leads, and legal-review questions remain unresolved until reviewed outside this template.

### Corpus And Chunk Workflow Boundaries

- Verify that corpus and chunk workflow references remain navigation, organization, and traceability references only.
- Verify that chunked corpus references do not prove source status, issue relevance, submitted status, legal sufficiency, or ownership.
- Verify that corpus excerpts are not copied into this template.

### Appendix / Submission Workflow Boundaries

- Verify that appendix candidates are not submitted evidence.
- Verify that submitted appendices are not proof by themselves.
- Verify that submission packages are not legal conclusions.
- Verify that appendix registers are navigation, not sufficiency.
- Verify that transmission or submission references prove at most that something was transmitted or submitted, not the truth of the underlying fact.

### Rebuttal Matrix Neutrality

- Verify that neither party's assertion is treated as truth by itself.
- Verify that admitted-or-overlapping facts still require source-status handling before core use.
- Verify that disputed factual assertions remain disputed until source-status and issue-relevance gates support later use.
- Verify that disputed inferences remain unresolved unless manually reviewed outside the matrix.
- Verify that rebuttal matrix organization is not proof, legal conclusion, sufficiency scoring, or ownership determination.

### Side-Track Separation

- Verify that bohag/lösöre remains separate from hidden-co-ownership core proof.
- Verify that item return requests, value-in-bodelning alternatives, item value claims, possession/access notes, retrieval/access logistics, item receipts, and item appendix references are not proof of hidden co-ownership.
- Verify that improvement-or-fixture ambiguity remains manual-review-required.
- Verify that side-track material does not enter hidden-co-ownership core unless later work separately source-backs and issue-classifies it to core under manual review.

- The closed-layer list is complete and internally coherent.
- The checklist preserves source-status, issue-relevance, appendix/submission, rebuttal-matrix, and side-track boundaries.
- The checklist preserves no sufficiency, no conclusion, and no implementation boundaries.
- The checklist contains only anonymized, reusable review prompts and requirements.
- The checklist contains no private case facts, raw private excerpts, real identifiers, exact private file paths, or copied private uploaded documents.
- The checklist creates no legal advice, legal decision logic, proof, ownership determination, runtime behavior, schema behavior, semantic-fact mapping, outcome prediction, or pleading generation.

- Verify that the template remains outside source-status classification, issue relevance classification, working-ledger workflow, corpus workflow, appendix workflow, rebuttal workflow, side-track workflow, full doctrine contract, runtime implementation, schema behavior, semantic-fact mapping, and legal decision logic.
